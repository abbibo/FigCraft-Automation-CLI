# FigCraft UX Writing and Microcopy Craft

Eliminates placeholder content and generic AI filler text. Authentic user experience requires realistic domain vocabulary, context-aware figures, and clear action-oriented microcopy.

## 1. Zero Placeholder Policy

Strictly banned in all FigCraft designs and mockups:
- No "Lorem Ipsum", "Dolor sit amet", or pseudo-Latin blocks.
- No "John Doe", "Jane Doe", "User 1", or "Test Name".
- No "Item 1", "Card Title", "Product Name", or "Sample Description".
- No unhelpful buttons labeled simply "Submit", "Click Here", or "Learn More".
- No generic marketing slogans like "Supercharge your workflow" or "Unleash infinite possibilities".

## 2. Action-First Button Microcopy

Every button and link must specify an explicit verb combined with an object:
- Instead of "Submit" -> "Create Project", "Save Changes", "Send Invoice".
- Instead of "OK" -> "Confirm Transfer", "Delete Workspace", "Archive Member".
- Instead of "Next" -> "Continue to Billing", "Review Order".
- Secondary actions: "Cancel", "Back to Projects", "Save as Draft".

## 3. Realistic Domain Data Seeds

Seed mockups with authentic, domain-appropriate records:

1. **Finance and FinTech**:
   - Realistic transaction descriptions: "Stripe Payout - Ref #89201", "AWS Cloud Infrastructure - Monthly", "GitHub Enterprise Seats".
   - Currency formatting with thousands separators: "$12,450.00", "EUR 4,890.50".
   - Status indicators: "Cleared", "Settling", "Failed - Insufficient Funds".
2. **Healthcare and Life Sciences**:
   - Patient names with realistic honorifics, medical records, blood pressure readings ("118/76 mmHg"), and lab test codes.
3. **E-Commerce and Retail**:
   - Concrete merchandise titles ("AeroMesh Running Shoes - Midnight Black / 10.5"), SKU numbers, and realistic shipping estimates ("Arrives Thursday, Nov 14").
4. **Developer Tools and SaaS**:
   - Realistic commit hashes ("e8f4a12"), branch names ("feature/auth-v2"), latency metrics ("142ms avg"), and error logs.

## 4. Helpful Error and Empty State Writing

1. **Empty States**: State what is missing, explain the benefit of adding it, and provide a direct path forward. Example: "No invoices yet. Create your first invoice to begin tracking client receivables."
2. **Form Errors**: State the exact requirement rather than blaming the user. Example: "Password must include at least 8 characters and one symbol."
